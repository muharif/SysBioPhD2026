### About the speaker

Rui Benfeitas is Head of Translational & Precision Medicine at Chiesi Pharma, where he leads work on chronic respiratory diseases including interstitial lung disease, pulmonary hypertension, COPD, asthma and bronchiectasis. He came to Sweden in 2016 as a postdoctoral researcher with the Human Protein Atlas and Sysmedicine at SciLifeLab and KTH Royal Institute of Technology, and from 2019 to 2022 served as Senior Bioinformatician and Vice Training Coordinator at the National Bioinformatics Infrastructure Sweden (NBIS), based at SciLifeLab and Stockholm University. His research develops biomarker strategies for patient stratification across early- and late-phase clinical research, combining systems medicine, network analysis and machine learning to integrate transcriptomic, proteomic, metabolomic and epigenomic data for disease characterisation and drug repositioning. He holds a PhD in Computational Biology from the University of Coimbra.
